Your local display

CarPlay, on a second screen.

Connect this browser to the DiPlay bridge on your Android device. Keep both devices on the same trusted private Wi-Fi network.

Before you connect

  1. Park safely. Enable the experimental browser bridge in DiPlay.
  2. Set the bridge’s allowed website origin to this page’s HTTPS origin.
  3. Enter its private IPv4 address and port below. Click Connect, allow Chrome’s local-network permission if you choose, then tap Accept in DiPlay on Android.

The local WebSocket link is unencrypted, including video, audio, and touch controls. Use only your own trusted private network. HTTPS protects this page, not that local link.

Private IPv4 only. Enter the address shown in DiPlay, without ws:// or a path. 1–65535. Every connection requires a fresh Accept on Android within 30 seconds.

Browser requirements & privacy

Open this page over HTTPS in Chrome 147 or later with WebCodecs and Local Network Access support. Embedded WebViews, older browsers, and many in-car browsers may not support this flow. Install the latest DiPlay APK and reload this viewer together: both must support Android approval (protocol v2). A supported codec is checked when the bridge sends its configuration. H.265 support depends on the browser and device.

If a connection is blocked, check the site’s local-network permission, the bridge’s allowed origin, and that both devices share a trusted LAN. Do not disable browser security, ignore certificate warnings, or expose the bridge to the internet.

Click Play audio here to move playback from Android to this browser. Stop browser audio to return playback to Android. Audio needs Web Audio and AudioWorklet support; it is experimental and is not a guarantee of synchronized audio/video or call support. This viewer has no analytics, third-party scripts, internet media uploads, recording, credential storage, or microphone access. Video and audio stay between the Android bridge and this browser. The parked checkbox is your confirmation; this page does not detect vehicle speed.
