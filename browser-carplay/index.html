Your local display

CarPlay, on a second screen.

Connect this browser to the DiPlay bridge on your Android device. Keep both devices on the same trusted private Wi-Fi network.

Before you connect

  1. Park safely. Enable the experimental browser bridge in DiPlay.
  2. Set the bridge’s allowed website origin to this page’s HTTPS origin.
  3. Enter its endpoint and temporary pairing token below. Click Connect, then allow Chrome’s local-network permission if you choose to proceed.

The local WebSocket link is unencrypted, including the token, video, and touch controls. Use only your own trusted private network. HTTPS protects this page, not that local link.

Copy the exact private IPv4 address and port shown in DiPlay. Used for this connection only. Never put it in a URL. Re-enter it to reconnect.

Checking this browser…

Your display will appear hereNo connection starts until you click Connect.

Touch is off until you enable it, with up to two simultaneous contacts. Switching tabs, locking the screen, or leaving this page disconnects. There is no automatic reconnect.

Browser requirements & privacy

Open this page over HTTPS in Chrome 147 or later with WebCodecs and Local Network Access support. Embedded WebViews, older browsers, and many in-car browsers may not support this flow. A supported codec is checked when the bridge sends its configuration. H.265 support depends on the browser and device.

If a connection is blocked, check the site’s local-network permission, the bridge’s allowed origin, and that both devices share a trusted LAN. Do not disable browser security, ignore certificate warnings, or expose the bridge to the internet.

This viewer has no analytics, third-party scripts, uploads, recording, credential storage, audio, or microphone access. Video stays between the Android bridge and this browser. The parked checkbox is your confirmation; this page does not detect vehicle speed.
